One house, two arrival times

If one family flies from Schiphol and another joins from Rotterdam or Eindhoven, send the flight number into Ibiza for each party. We’ll check who needs child seats, who has the villa host’s number, and who can meet the driver if the first guests arrive before the keys are ready.

For example, an early group might want lunch near the villa while the later group goes straight to the house. We check bag storage with the host before quoting that plan. You can then compare separate pickups with having a car on call.

If you’re still choosing the departure airport, check Transavia’s routes to Ibiza for your dates. If you’re still choosing the house, explore our Ibiza villas and concierge services.

What should be ready when you walk in?

For a private chef, tell us how many guests will eat at the house, any dietary requests, and whether you’d like a shopping list quoted. We’ll check kitchen access with the host and separate ingredients from the chef’s hours in the proposal.

If some guests want to go out and others stay in, choose one fixed plan first. That might be the boat’s boarding time, lunch in Santa Gertrudis, or a dinner reservation. The chauffeur schedule can then be quoted for the people who actually need it.
