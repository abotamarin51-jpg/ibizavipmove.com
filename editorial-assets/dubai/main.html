Before the household arrives

If you’ve already chosen a villa, tell us when the host can hand over the keys and which guests arrive first. We can coordinate the chauffeur schedule with a private chef or villa staff, check a proposed menu and establish what should be ready for the first meal.

If the villa search is still open, share bedroom arrangements, any accessibility needs and the areas where the group wants to spend time. We’ll check these details against specific properties before proposing one.

For a private flight, your handling agent’s confirmed meeting instructions determine the pickup point. If other guests arrive at the passenger terminal, we can plan the two pickups separately.
